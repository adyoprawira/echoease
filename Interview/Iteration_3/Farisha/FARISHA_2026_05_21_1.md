## Interview 1 

**Recording Link:**[Listen to Interview 1](https://uq.sharepoint.com/:u:/r/teams/Section_7620_22283/Shared%20Documents/54_Richard/Iteration%203_Interview_Nabiha%20Farisha/Iteration%203_Interview%201_Nabiha%20Farisha.m4a?csf=1&web=1&e=OrIGrd)

|   **Questions**   |   **Responses**   |
|What would you click if you were stressed today?| I would click “Explore Stories” because they like reading posts from people who share similar struggles. It helps me feel understood and find others from the same course going through similar issues.|
|Do the tags help you find relevant posts?| Yes. I felt the tags were helpful as long as I can relate to what they’re currently struggling with. It would be better if the tags could include academic subjects, not just mental‑health topics.| 
|Did you feel safe posting this?| Yes. I felt safe because the feature is anonymous, similar to anonymous EdDiscussion boards (for academic questions) or a “stan Twitter” account where I can express myself freely without revealing their identity.|
|What influenced your choice of professional to contact?|I would choose a professional based on their area of specialisation, not just availability. If my preferred specialist isn’t available, I would then choose whoever is available.|
|Did the activities while waiting for queue help?| Yes. The waiting‑time activities (lo‑fi music, piano, nature music, breathing activities) as fun mental‑health exercises that help them relax while waiting.|
|Which resource feels most relevant to you?| All resources are useful depending on their needs. I think after‑hours support is the most important because most services close at 5 PM, and people may need help outside working hours.|
|Did this 'emergency helpline section' feel urgent enough?| I understood the emergency box, but I reckon it did not stand out enough. I think if you use a more urgent colour like red or yellow instead of purple can increase visibility.|
|Are there any features you wish we had?| They felt like the system was mostly complete. My only suggestion is to improve the emergency helpline box to make it more noticeable.|
