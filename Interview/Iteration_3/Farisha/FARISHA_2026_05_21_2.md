|   **Questions**   |   **Responses**   |
|What would you click first if you were stressed today?|I would immediately click the emergency number (Triple Zero) in the crisis box because that feels like the most urgent and appropriate option when stressed.| 
|Do the tags help you find relevant posts?|Yes. I felt the tags were highly relevant, especially hashtags like #studytips, #examanxiety, and #exams, which match what most students experience during exam season.|
|Did you feel safe posting this?|Yes. The platform feels safe because the purpose is to share feelings, and the anonymous posting option makes me comfortable expressing themselves honestly.|
|What influenced your choice of professional to contact?|I would choose someone available now if I urgently needed help. If it wasn’t urgent, I would choose someone in session or away. I appreciated having different availability statuses so they know whether they can talk to someone immediately or need to wait.|
|Did the activities while waiting for queue help?|Yes. I felt the waiting‑time activities (box breathing, journaling, lo‑fi/piano audio) would help distract me from anxiety and stop them from focusing on the waiting time.|
|Which resource feels most relevant to you?|The health clinic would be the most relevant resource for them. I would usually talk to friends before seeking counselling or peer support, but having a medical clinic available would be a big help.|
|Did this feel urgent enough?|Yes. I think the after‑hours care feature communicated urgency well, especially because it’s available 24/7. It would be helpful during late‑night stress when friends and family are unavailable.|
|Are there any features you wish we had?|I like all current features. T
My only suggestion was adding a personal profile where students can track when they feel most stressed (e.g., assignment weeks, exam periods). This could help professionals prepare more sessions during peak stress times and allow students to understand their own stress patterns.|
