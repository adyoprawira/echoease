## Interview 2

**Recording link**: [Listen to Interview 2]()


| **Question** | **Answer** |
| --- | --- |
| What are your first impressions of the landing page? | If I needed help with stress, I’d go straight to the Resources and Support section. The Mindfulness Pulse also stands out because it shows progress and monthly tracking, which feels motivating and useful. |
| What would be the first thing you click on? | I’d click on Resources first, since it feels like the most direct place to find immediate support or guidance. |
| Do the trained professionals feel trustworthy? | Yes, they seem trustworthy. I probably wouldn’t go to them immediately, though. I’d prefer to try the chat first and then maybe talk to someone in person later if needed. |
| How would you use the community forum? | I’d mostly read other people’s stories. I might reply to a few posts if I feel like I can offer advice or support, but I wouldn’t post my own experiences. |
| What do you think of the chat queue and breathing exercise feature?** | The breathing exercise seems helpful for calming down, and the queue system is clear with the wait time and position. The colours and layout feel soothing, and the lo‑fi music adds a nice comforting touch. |
| Does the emergency resources section feel urgent enough? | The purple colour helps it stand out, but it could be even more noticeable if placed at the very top of the page. Right now, it blends in with other elements except for the colour. |
| Any additional feedback or improvements? | Making urgent resources the first thing users see would help. Overall, the design is calming and supportive, but clearer prioritisation could improve usability. |
