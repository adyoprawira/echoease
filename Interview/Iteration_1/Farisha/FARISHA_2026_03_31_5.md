| Hi, my name is Farisha, and I am a student of COMP1100. I am currently doing an interview for my research assignment, and my question will be based on your well-being. Are you okay with that? | Yeap. |
| Okay, this is a recording interview, and can you introduce yourself please? | I'm *********. |
| Okay, so how do you manage your time juggling between school and your personal matter? | I try to finish my school stuff first, then I will have time to do personal stuff. Yeap, but sometimes I get backtracked. |
| So, what do you do to get yourself out of your stress? | I just watch TV, listen to music. |
| So, is there any point in your life that makes you feel like giving up and why? | Yeah, when I'm really behind on an assignment or behind on my study for an exam. Or if I haven't had anything enjoyable to do in a long time. |
| Yeah, so when you are struggling with stress at 2 in the morning, where do you turn first? Who is the first person you will go to? | I think probably text one of my close friends. |
| So, do you think the school response helps you at that hour? | I don't think so. I feel like they add to the stress. |
| So, do you prefer to be alone or talking to someone when you are not mentally in a good condition? | Probably talking to someone. |
| Yeah, why do you think so? | I think most of the time if I'm not in a happy mood, it's because I haven't had human interaction. I'm the unhappiest when I'm on my own because the bad thoughts snowball into anxiety and stuff. |
| Yeah, that's fair. So, do you feel like you have enough support around you at the moment? | Yeah, I realise so. |
| So, did you have any financial problems so far and how do you tackle that issue? | Not really major financial problems, but just trying to save up. I'm trying to find a better job. |
| So, on a scale of 1 to 10, how stressed are you about your debts or financial management? | I would say I'm not too stressed. About a 5. |
| So, in your opinion, what would make a support service feel safe enough for you to open up for the first time? | Maybe if they have something that's all-welcome. I don't go to support services because my debts aren't that big. But if they had financial advice or how to deal with things, I would maybe go and ask questions. |
| If it's about your mental condition, what do you think about it? | I feel like they usually have resources and stuff, so that's useful. They have places you can go to seek support, like hotlines or texts. That's really useful, I think. |
| Yeap, so do you work part-time jobs? | Yes. |
| So, what's stopping you from asking uni for money? | I feel like other students deserve it more. I don't want to take their money. |
| So, do you prefer to talk to counsellors or take online quizzes? | I feel like talking would be better. Online quizzes just ask “are you happy?” But if you talk to someone, they can give advice instead of just saying “you’re unhappy.” |
| Alright, that's cool. Okay, thank you so much. | Yes, all good. |
