## Interview 5 (Second Year in Computer Science, International Student)

| **Question** | **Answer** |
|--------------|------------|
| Can you introduce your name and age? | My name is ****, and I will be 21 soon. |
| What degree are you taking right now? | I am currently studying a Bachelor of Computer Science, majoring in Cyber Security. |
| Did you give your consent for this recording? | Yes, I give my consent. |
| How do you manage your time between school and personal matters? | I rely on a physical planner because I can see the spread of my week, including how busy I am and how much free time I have. I also categorise my tasks using colour‑coded pens, such as pink for classes and blue for daily life tasks like laundry, cooking, or buying groceries. |
| What do you do to manage your stress? | Sometimes I go to sleep, and if that does not work, I buy myself something nice, like a good coffee from a place I have never tried before, as a form of self‑reward. |
| Do you prefer being alone when you are stressed? | It depends. I usually isolate myself first until I feel better or ready to talk. After that, I reach out to someone I trust — usually my best friend — to process my feelings better. |
| Do you feel like you have enough support around you? | Yes, I believe I do. I have supportive friends who are willing to listen, comfort me, and sometimes even give constructive criticism, which I accept. |
| Do you have any financial problems? | Not really, although money can be a bit tight at times. My allowance is not very high, which can be slightly stressful, but I am still managing and getting by. |
| On a scale from 1 to 10, how stressed are you about your financial situation? | I would rate it around 4. |
| Have you ever reached out to university support services? | Yes, once for an assignment extension, but I have not tried counselling services. |
| If you are stressed at 2 a.m., who do you turn to? | I would turn to my friends, as they are more likely to be awake at that time compared to family or university services. |
| If given a choice between talking to a counsellor or taking an anonymous stress quiz, which would you choose? | I would choose talking to a counsellor because expressing my feelings helps me organise my thoughts, feel less alone, and possibly receive helpful solutions. |
| Do you feel safe opening up to university support services? | Yes, I feel safe because they are expected to protect my privacy and keep my information confidential. |
