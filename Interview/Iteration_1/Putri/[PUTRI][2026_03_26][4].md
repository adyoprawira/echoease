## Interview 4 (Second Year in Computer Science, International Student)

**Recording link**: [Listen to Interview 4](https://uq.sharepoint.com/:v:/r/teams/Section_7620_22283/Shared%20Documents/54_Richard/Interview%204_Putri%20Anadamia.mp4?csf=1&web=1&e=ddLbzp)


| **Question** | **Answer** |
|--------------|------------|
| Can you introduce your name and age? | Hi, my name is ******************, and I am currently 23 years old. |
| What degree are you taking right now? | I am currently doing my Bachelor’s in Computer Science, majoring in Data Science. |
| Did you give your consent for this recording? | Yes, I fully give my consent. |
| How do you manage your time between school and personal matters? | During weekends, I fully dedicate my time to personal matters. However, during weekdays, it is slightly imbalanced as I focus more on school. At night, I usually log off from everything and rest. |
| What do you do to manage your stress? | I like sweet treats, so I usually go out to get something sweet to eat or socialise with my friends outside. |
| Do you prefer being alone when you are stressed? | I prefer to be alone, but when things feel too heavy, I would want at least one person to listen to me without necessarily giving feedback. |
| Do you feel like you have enough support around you? | I have enough personal support, but when it comes to university support, although there are many systems available, I find it confusing to navigate as I get referred to different places. |
| Do you have any financial problems? | No, I do not have financial problems. However, I feel that I am not financially literate enough. |
| On a scale from 1 to 10, how stressed are you about your financial situation? | I would rate it around 5 to 6. It is not exactly stress, but more about not being fully aware of my daily spending and financial management. |
| If you are stressed at 2 a.m., who do you turn to? | I would turn to my friends or family members first because they are more accessible. University support services follow office hours, so by the time they are available, the feeling might have already passed. |
| If given a choice between talking to a counsellor or taking an anonymous stress quiz, which would you choose? | I would choose to talk to a counsellor because when I feel down, I would not want to take a quiz. |
| Would you find it helpful to have access to a university support link when stressed? | Yes, that would be helpful. |
| Do you feel safe enough to open up to university support services? | I would still have my guard up. I understand that I need to be honest for them to help me, but I would not be fully emotional and would keep things more professional. |
