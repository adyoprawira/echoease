| **Questions** | **Responses** |
|---------------|----------------|
| How do you manage your time juggling between school and your personal matters? | I usually manage my time by looking at my timetable. I plan my schedule based on when I need to attend classes. After that, I allocate time for personal activities such as relaxing or going out for a run. |
| What do you do to get yourself out of your stress? | I usually take time to relax, such as walking around South Bank or doing physical activities like running. These help relieve stress. |
| Do you have any financial problems so far and how do you tackle that issue? | I have not faced major financial problems, but I do manage expenses like eating out with friends. I use a finance tracking app called Budget to monitor my spending and stay on track. |
| Do you prefer to be alone or talking to someone when you are not in a good condition mentally? | I prefer talking to someone. I usually reach out to a friend because human connection helps relieve stress and problems. |
| Do you feel like you have enough support around you at the moment? | Yes, I feel I have enough support. I have friends I can talk to, and I am also aware that the university provides support services if needed. |
| On a scale of one to ten, how stressed are you about your debt? | Around 7 to 8. It is quite stressful because as a student from New Zealand, I cannot access student loans. I am currently trying to find a part‑time job to manage my finances better. |
