| **Questions** | **Responses** |
|---------------|----------------|
| How do you manage your time juggling between school and your personal matters? | Currently, I find it quite manageable because I have two weekdays off (Tuesday and Wednesday). I use that time to relax and do personal activities like playing games, reading books, and resting, which helps me maintain balance. |
| What do you do to get yourself out of stress? | I do not get stressed often, but when I do, I prefer to be alone. I usually rest, sleep, or watch movies to take my mind off things. |
| Do you have any financial problems so far and how do you tackle that issue? | I do have some financial challenges as an international student because tuition fees are high due to currency exchange differences. For daily life, I manage by cooking instead of eating out. For tuition, I rely on my parents and am currently trying to find a part‑time job to help support them. |
| Do you prefer to be alone or talking to someone when you are not in a good condition mentally? | I prefer to be alone. When I feel tired or mentally drained, I like to take time for myself rather than talk to others. |
| Do you feel like you have enough support around you at the moment? | Yes, I feel supported. I live with five roommates who are also my close friends, and I can rely on them if I need help. |
