| **Questions** | **Responses** |
|---------------|----------------|
| How do you manage your time juggling between school and your personal matters? | I do not have a fixed method, but I usually use a to‑do list and set schedules on my calendar. This helps me keep track of what I need to do for the day and the following days. |
| What do you do to get yourself out of your stress? | I usually sleep, watch movies or Netflix, hang out with friends, or go to dance classes. Dancing especially helps me release stress. |
| Do you have any financial problems so far and how do you tackle that issue? | I do not currently have financial problems, as my parents mainly support my finances. I also work, but I am not fully responsible for financial matters yet. |
| Do you prefer to be alone or talking to someone when you are not in a good condition mentally? | It depends on the situation. Sometimes I prefer to be alone, but other times I need to talk to a friend and share my feelings. |
| Do you feel like you have enough support around you at the moment? | Yes, I feel supported by my friends and also by university staff who are willing to help with mental health concerns. |
| On a scale of one to ten, how stressed are you about your debt? | Around 1. I do not have personal debt, and my finances are mostly handled by my parents. |
