| **Questions** | **Responses** |
|---------------|----------------|
| How do you manage your time juggling between school and your personal matters? | I manage my time by setting priorities. If I have many assignments, I focus on completing school tasks first. After finishing them, I allow myself to do personal activities. |
| What do you do to get yourself out of your stress? | When I feel stressed, I prefer to talk to someone. Having someone to talk to helps me manage stress. |
| Do you have any financial problems so far and how do you tackle that issue? | I had financial problems in the past due to a lavish lifestyle. However, I found a job with good pay and learned how to manage and save money better. |
| Do you prefer to be alone or talking to someone when you are not in a good condition mentally? | I prefer talking to someone, especially someone I feel comfortable with. |
| Do you feel like you have enough support around you at the moment? | Yes, I feel that I have enough support around me. |
| On a scale of one to ten, how stressed are you about your debt? | Around 2. I am confident I can manage my debt, and I have improved my lifestyle to be less financially risky. |
