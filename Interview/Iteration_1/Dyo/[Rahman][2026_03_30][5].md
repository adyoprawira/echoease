| How do you manage your time juggling between school and personal matters? | I have some activities like tennis and tournaments, and sometimes they clash with my tutorials. For example, I might have a tutorial at 5:56 PM and a tournament at 5 PM, so sometimes I skip one for the other. It’s kind of up and down, but I try to balance it in the end. |
| What do you do to get yourself out of stress? | I have hobbies like playing games and piano. If I get stressed, I go to my senior’s room and play piano to release stress, then continue studying or doing assignments. |
| Is there any point in your life that makes you feel like giving up and why? | I don’t want to answer that. |
| When you're struggling with stress at 2 AM, where do you turn first? Does school response help you at that hour? | It depends on my mood. If I feel religious, I pray. If not, I scroll TikTok or play casual games to relieve stress, then continue my work. |
| Do you prefer to be alone or talking to someone when you're not in a good mental condition? | I prefer talking to trusted friends. I call them, tell them how I feel, and follow their advice if it works. |
| Do you feel like you have enough support around you at the moment? | I’m the type who handles problems myself. Even if I have friends, I usually solve my problems on my own. |
| Did you have any financial problems so far and how do you tackle that issue? | Yes, I live on a limited budget. I try to save money by cooking instead of eating out and sometimes reducing meals so I can afford other things like clothes. |
| Do you currently have any debt? | I don’t think so, I don’t have debt. |
| In your opinion, what would make a support service feel safe enough to open up to on the first try? | I’ve never used support services. I usually handle problems on my own, so I’m not really sure. |
| Do you prefer to talk to counselors or take online quizzes? | I prefer counselors because I can speak openly and get advice from someone experienced. |
| What is stopping you from asking the university for financial help? | I’ve never asked. I just find part-time jobs instead, even though it’s tiring, but the money is worth it. |
