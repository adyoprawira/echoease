| **Questions** | **Responses** |
|---------------|----------------|
| How do you manage your time juggling between school and personal matters? | I use apps like Google Calendar to organize my schedule and manage both school and personal time. |
| What do you do to get yourself out of stress? | I exercise and play music, like guitar. |
| Did you have any financial problems so far and how do you tackle that issue? | Not really. I try to use my money wisely and manage my expenses carefully. |
| Do you prefer to be alone or talking to someone when you're not in a good mental condition? | I prefer to be alone and reflect on the problem so I can solve it myself. |
| Do you feel like you have enough support around you at the moment? | Yes, I have support from friends and family. My parents check in on me regularly. |
| On a scale of 1–10, how stressed are you about your debt? | Around 1. |
| Have you ever looked for help from university support services? Why/why not? | No, I haven’t. I don’t really believe they would be helpful. |
| When you are stressed late at night, where do you usually turn for help? | I deal with it by myself rather than seeking help from the university. |
