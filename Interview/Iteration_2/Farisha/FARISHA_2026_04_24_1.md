## Interview 1

| **Questions** | **Responses** |
|---------------|---------------|
|Do you know that UQ Student Hub on Discord exists?| I don't know |
|What do you think of the kind of forums the channel provides there? | Maybe some resources taht would be helpful for students going through things, like mental health. |
| Let’s say you are having a problem mentally, when you imagine a “Support” button directly inside your UQ Blackboard, what do you expect it to do or show?| I expected it to be able to provide resources, and assume that I'm not sure how to start, so probably like starting right from the beginning of reaching out; like resources(space), people to talk to(space), and that sort of thing.|
|Does it matter to you if the person on the other end is a trained professional or a fellow student who has been through similar units?| I think either would be good. Probably a trained professional would be like easier to trust, but also people that have gone through it like, they would understand. So it's also very useful.|
Why do you think a trained professional is better in catering to your problems/ answering you? | They have been trained to deal with that sort of thing, so they know how to help when they need the right answers. |
|If you are reading on the community forum that fellow peers are having the same situation as you, how would you feel?|I think it is easy to relate to someone that is going through the same thing, some sort of circumstances, especially being a student kind of thing.| 
|If we provide this service without requiring you to make a booking (zero effort), do you still want to cope with your problem by yourself or would you talk through it with someone?|  I think if I was struggling to much I would definitely move forward with it and use the platform because if it is super easy to access, then it kind of takes the stress out of having to find how to do it, but if you just go, this time, this day, it got to make life a lot easier.|
| What is the maximum amount of time you would be willing to wait for a response before you went back to your usual coping habits?| Probably within 24 hours, because if you are going to waith for 4 days, then you kinda start looking for other resources or kind of start reverting back to things.|
|What would an ideal effortless support experience look like for you?| Maybe something like a group scenario, as in like a group of some people going through the same thing, where there also a kind of trained professional present, that you can kinda ask questions and bounce off each other an learn from each other's experience. |
