## Interview 4
 **Recording link**: [Listen to Interview 4](https://uq.sharepoint.com/:u:/r/teams/Section_7620_22283/Shared%20Documents/54_Richard/Iteration%202_Interview%204_Farisha.m4a?csf=1&web=1&e=xD5QT2)

| **Questions** | **Responses** |
|---------------|---------------|
|Do you know that UQ Student Hub on Discord exists?| No I do not.|
|What do you think of the kind of forums the channel provides there? | I have no idea what’s on it, but if it included helpful sites or references for support, that would be good.|
| Let’s say you are having a problem mentally, when you imagine a “Support” button directly inside your UQ Blackboard, what do you expect it to do or show?|I’d expect it to show links to helpful sites, especially for counselling or support services.|
|Does it matter to you if the person on the other end is a trained professional or a fellow student who has been through similar units?|I prefer a trained professional. I wouldn’t really prefer talking to a student.|
Why do you think a trained professional is better in catering to your problems/ answering you? |Because having the same experience doesn’t mean people cope the same way. Professionals know how to handle things properly and understand different situations better than peers.|
|If you are reading on the community forum that fellow peers are having the same situation as you, how would you feel?|I would feel connected to them.| 
|If we provide this service without requiring you to make a booking (zero effort), do you still want to cope with your problem by yourself or would you talk through it with someone?|It depends on the topic. I’d like to talk to a professional, but there are some things I’d be afraid to share. If it’s easy to access, it would be better.|
| What is the maximum amount of time you would be willing to wait for a response before you went back to your usual coping habits?| Maybe up to a week. Earlier is better, but sometimes people don’t check emails often. A week would be the maximum.|
|What would an ideal effortless support experience look like for you?|I reach out, they respond, and the appointment is set easily. A simple process, a comfortable environment, and a one‑on‑one session with open‑minded people. Being anonymous is also important.|
