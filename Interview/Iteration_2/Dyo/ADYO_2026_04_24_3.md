## Interview 3

**Recording link**: [Listen to Interview 3](https://uq.sharepoint.com/:u:/r/teams/Section_7620_22283/Shared%20Documents/54_Richard/Iteration%202_Interview%203_Adyo%20Arkan%20Prawira.m4a?csf=1&web=1&e=IdLuuL)

| Questions | Responses |
|----------|-----------|
| Do you know that the UQ student hub on Discord exists? | No. |
| If you were to take a guess, what do you think of the kind of forum that the channel provides there? | Mankind, support, I don't know. |
| When you imagine a support button directly inside your Blackboard, what do you expect it to do or show? | It will have a link that can help me solve my problems. If I need help in an assignment, it will direct me to contact the TAs or the lecturer. |
| Does it matter if the person on the other end is a trained professional or a fellow student who has been through similar units? | Maybe both, but I will most likely take advice from the professionals. I will take the things that fellow students have taken into consideration as well. |
| If you're reading on a community forum that fellow peers are in the same situation as you, how would you feel? | I would try to give some advice anonymously, saying I've been in that boat and this material helped me pass this course. |
| If we provided a support service without requiring you to make a booking, would you talk through it with someone? | I'll say talk to it with someone. |
| What is the maximum amount of time you would be willing to wait for a response? | Maybe an hour, because what we expect from a support hotline is a quick response. |
| What would an ideal effortless support experience look like for you? | Something where we could talk to someone 1-to-1 or give our thoughts in a forum where people can help each other. For the 1-on-1s, it can be a more deep dive into your problems. |
