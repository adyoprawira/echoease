## Interview 5

**Recording link**: [Listen to Interview 5](https://uq.sharepoint.com/:u:/r/teams/Section_7620_22283/Shared%20Documents/54_Richard/Iteration%202_Interview%205_Adyo%20Arkan%20Prawira.m4a?csf=1&web=1&e=tMDeCJ)

| Questions | Responses |
|----------|-----------|
| Do you know that the UQ student hub on Discord exists? | I didn't even know they have a Discord. |
| If you can take a guess, what kind of channels do they provide? | I assume they have a ticket channel where you open a ticket and say you have a problem, or maybe counseling, or talks/announcements on self-improvement and mental health. |
| When you imagine a support button directly inside your Blackboard, what would you want it to show? | When I click on support, preferably when it comes to mental health, it's nicer to have someone to be able to actively interact with you. A live chat where you can start a conversation and they reply to you. |
| Does it matter if the person on the other end is a trained professional or a fellow student? | For general advice, it doesn't matter. But for something personal or sensitive, I'd prefer a professional. If you talk to a student, they might misunderstand and give bad advice. |
| What is the maximum amount of time you would be willing to wait for a response? | 1 hour max. |
| What would an ideal effortless support experience look like for you? | The idea of an AI experience because when you ask a question, it instantly gives you a response. However, human interaction will always be better than texting because you can understand more of how people feel and you can infer. If calling is possible, that would be even better. |
