## Interview 2

**Recording link**: [Listen to Interview 2](https://uq.sharepoint.com/:u:/r/teams/Section_7620_22283/Shared%20Documents/54_Richard/Iteration%202_Interview%202_Adyo%20Arkan%20Prawira.m4a?csf=1&web=1&e=7Ev9eB)

| Questions | Responses |
|----------|-----------|
| Do you know that the UQ student hub on Discord exists? | I think I've heard of it, but I'm not really familiar with it. |
| If you were to take a guess, what kind of forums or channels does it provide? | My guess would be something along the lines of a channel for meeting new people or channels for discussing courses. Maybe also a channel for clubs. Mostly topics of discussion about UQ. |
| When you imagine a support button directly inside your Blackboard, what do you expect it to do or show? | It would show some contacts for people who we can reach out to, or maybe if the person having problems is not very comfortable talking to other people, also some resources. |
| Does it matter if the person on the other end is a trained professional or a fellow student? | I wouldn't mind either. Students are more down-to-earth and relatable, while professionals are helpful because they know exactly what to do. |
| If you're reading on a community forum that fellow peers are in the same situation as you, how would you feel? | It's a nice read. It makes you feel like you aren't the only one and might give people more confidence to reach out. |
| What is the maximum amount of time you would be willing to wait for a response before you went back to your usual coping habits? | For some people, they can wait for an hour or two, or maybe a day or two until they can meet someone. It depends on their personal preference. |
| What would an ideal effortless support experience look like for you? | A system more tailor-made for the students. Dividing into sections based on waiting times and then based on experiences. Maybe they prefer to talk to a student counselor who has gone through a similar experience, or maybe they prefer a professional. It ensures they feel more heard. |
