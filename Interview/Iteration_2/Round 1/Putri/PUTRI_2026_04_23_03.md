| **Question** | **Answer** |
|--------------|------------|
| Do you know that the UQ Student Hub on Discord exists? | Yes, I’ve heard about it. |
| What do you think about creating a mental health forum within the UQ Student Hub on Discord? | I think that would be good. |
| Would you be more likely to read others’ stories or actively post your own? | I would read others’ stories rather than post my own. |
| When you imagine a “Support” button inside Blackboard, what do you expect it to do or show? | (Unclear / unsure response.) |
| Does it matter if the support is from a trained professional or a fellow student? | No, I don’t think it matters. |
| Do you think a peer or professional is better at helping? | A fellow student could give advice based on similar experiences, so it feels more relatable. |
| How would you feel reading a community forum where peers share similar struggles? | I would feel comforted knowing others are going through similar situations. |
| If support required zero effort (no booking), would you talk to someone or cope alone? | I would talk to someone. |
| What is the maximum time you would wait for a response before going back to your usual coping habits? | About one day. |
| What would an ideal effortless support experience look like? | Something easily accessible without needing to book or schedule. Scheduling feels like effort because you need to plan your week. |
