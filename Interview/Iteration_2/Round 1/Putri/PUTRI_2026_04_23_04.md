| **Question** | **Answer** |
|--------------|------------|
| Do you know that the UQ Student Hub on Discord exists? | Yes, I know it exists, but I don’t really use it much. |
| What do you think about creating a mental health forum within the UQ Student Hub on Discord? | It would be good, especially if it can be anonymous. |
| Would you be more likely to read others’ stories or actively post your own? | Both. I’d like to read others’ experiences to see if people are going through the same thing, and sometimes I would also post my own problems to get advice. |
| When you imagine a “Support” button inside Blackboard, what do you expect it to do or show? | Something like a forum where you can post your thoughts or problems anonymously and get responses, solutions, or new ideas from others. |
| Does it matter if the support is from a trained professional or a fellow student? | Both are fine, but it would be better if the first interaction is with a student, and then if things get worse, it can be referred to a professional. |
| Why do you think a peer is better initially? | It feels like a safer and more comfortable environment since they are around the same age and have similar experiences. |
| How would you feel reading a community forum where peers share similar struggles? | It would be okay. I could understand others’ struggles and maybe even help them as well. |
| If support required zero effort (no booking), would you talk to someone or cope alone? | I usually solve problems by myself, but if I need help, I would prefer to talk to someone. |
| What is the maximum time you would wait for a response before going back to your usual coping habits? | Around 4 to 5 days. |
| What would an ideal effortless support experience look like? | Immediate or last-minute support, like a hotline or night service. Something accessible anytime, especially during late hours when problems often feel more intense. |
