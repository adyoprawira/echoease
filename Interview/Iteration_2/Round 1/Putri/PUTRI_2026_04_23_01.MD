## Interview 1 

**Recording link**: [Listen to Interview 1](https://uq.sharepoint.com/:v:/r/teams/Section_7620_22283/Shared%20Documents/54_Richard/Iteration%202_Interview_Putri/Interview%201.mp4?csf=1&web=1&e=dVki5d)


| **Question** | **Answer** |
|--------------|------------|
| Do you know that the UQ Student Hub on Discord exists? | No. |
| What do you think the kind of forums or services there would provide? | Probably support services for students, like mental health services, study support, and maybe international student services. |
| When you imagine a “Support” button inside Blackboard, what do you expect it to do or show? | A range of options like phone numbers, email contacts, or ways to meet someone in person on campus. Ideally, being able to email someone and talk to them over time or meet a few times during the semester. In-person support would help the most. |
| Does it matter if the support is from a trained professional or a fellow student? | It depends on the situation. For academic stress, someone with similar experience (like a senior or graduate) would help. For more serious mental health issues, a trained professional would be better. |
| Why is a peer or professional better in those situations? | A peer can give practical tips and relatable advice, while a professional can provide deeper, long-term strategies for mental health support. |
| How would you feel reading a forum where peers share similar struggles? | It would feel good and less lonely, but still less preferred compared to in-person interaction. Forums can feel distant since it's behind a screen. |
| If support required zero effort (no booking), would you talk to someone or cope alone? | It would be better to talk to someone. Lower effort would make students more likely to reach out. |
| What would an ideal effortless support experience look like? | A casual, informal meet-up where you can just talk, rather than a formal appointment or booking system. |
| What is the maximum time you would wait for a response before going back to your usual coping habits? | Maybe a couple of weeks, but not entirely sure. |
