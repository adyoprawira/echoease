## Interview 4

**Recording link**: [Listen to Interview 4](https://uq.sharepoint.com/:u:/r/teams/Section_7620_22283/Shared%20Documents/54_Richard/Iteration%202_%20Interview%204_Adyo%20Arkan%20Prawira.m4a?csf=1&web=1&e=Ah4eCq)

| Questions | Responses |
|----------|-----------|
| Do you know that the UQ student hub on Discord exists? | Yeah, I've seen it. |
| What do you think if we were to create a mental care forum within the UQ student hub? | I think it'd be an interesting idea. It would be a good way to help people connect. Having students be able to talk to someone or at least having a way to relieve stress with other people, I think it would be a good thing. |
| Would you be more likely to use it as someone who reads other stories or as an active participant posting your own? | Probably the former, as a person reading stories. I could see myself giving a helping hand. I don't see a reason for me to tell my story since life's been good, but I'd like to read other people's stories. |
| When you imagine a support button directly inside your Blackboard, what do you expect it to do or show? | My first thought was support on how to use Blackboard, but if it's for mental health, it could be like a link. It would be a very quick and easy button that a lot of people would use. |
| Does it matter if the person on the other end is a trained professional or a fellow student who has been through similar units? | A trained professional would be nice because there is a guarantee that they know how to help us the best. |
| If we provided a support service without requiring a booking or attending multiple sessions, would you think that helped reduce the friction? | I think making as little commitment as possible makes it very welcoming. Sometimes people can feel like they're trapped if they have to commit to something, especially something personal. I think that's a good idea; it can really help people. |
| Does creating the forum on Discord lessen the time required for people to get help? | Not necessarily. I do think it makes it more accessible because Discord is widely used. I don't know about speeding up the process, but given it's on Discord, it's more welcoming. |
