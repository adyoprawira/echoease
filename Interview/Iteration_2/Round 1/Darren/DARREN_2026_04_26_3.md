| Do you know that the UQ Student Hub on Discord exists? | I actually did not know that it exists. |
| What do you think of the kind of forums the channel provides there? | I am not sure what kind of forums it provides because I did not know it existed. |
| Let’s say you are having a problem mentally; when you imagine a “Support” button directly inside your UQ Blackboard, what do you expect it to do or show? | I would expect it to show hotline numbers for mental health services, crisis hotlines, and general low-commitment support options. Something casual and easy to access. |

### Section 2: Community vs. Automation

| **Questions** | **Responses** |
|---------------|----------------|
| Does it matter to you if the person on the other end is a trained professional or a fellow student who has been through similar units? | I think both have their place. A trained professional is better equipped to handle serious situations or crises, while a fellow student can provide shared experience and more relatable advice. |
| Why do you think a trained professional or peer is better at catering to your problems or answering you? | A trained professional has the education and expertise to deal with serious mental health problems. However, peers may better understand the relatability of being a student and facing similar pressures. |
| If you are reading on a community forum that fellow peers are in the same situation as you, how would you feel? | I would feel less alone and gain a sense of community, knowing that I am not the only one facing these kinds of problems. It would feel comforting. |

### Section 3: Effortless Intervention (Zero-Friction)

| **Questions** | **Responses** |
|---------------|----------------|
| If we provided a support service without requiring you to make a booking (zero effort), would you still prefer to cope with your problem by yourself, or would you talk through it with someone? | It really depends. Personally, I have always preferred to deal with problems by myself, so I am not entirely sure if I would use the service. |
| What is the maximum amount of time you would be willing to wait for a response before you went back to your usual coping habits? | It depends on how I am feeling. If I am in crisis, I would need support as soon as possible. For general stress, maybe four to five hours would be the maximum before I disregard the service and return to my usual coping habits. |
| What would an ideal "effortless" support experience look like for you? | It should be confidential, so what I share is not spread to others. The person or service should also be understanding of my situation without immediately judging me. |
