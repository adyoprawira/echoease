### Section 1: Integrated Access (Blackboard & Discord)

| **Questions** | **Responses** |
|---------------|----------------|
| Do you know that the UQ Student Hub on Discord exists? | Yes, I am pretty sure it exists. During orientation, tutors mentioned that students could use UQ support services if they needed help. |
| What do you think if we were to create a mental health care forum within the UQ Student Hub on Discord? | I think it would be great. It could make support easier to access, so students would not need to queue up and could register through the platform itself. |
| Would you be more likely to use it as someone who reads others' stories or as an active participant posting your own stories? Why? | If I was feeling down or overwhelmed with assignments, I could definitely use the platform. I am not sure about posting stories, but I think the features would be useful. |
| Let’s say you are having a problem mentally; when you imagine a “Support” button directly inside your Blackboard, what do you expect it to do or show? | I would expect it to guide me on how to get help, allow me to register my details, show where I need to go, who I would talk to, whether the person is qualified, and the available times. |

### Section 2: Community vs. Automation

| **Questions** | **Responses** |
|---------------|----------------|
| Does it matter to you if the person on the other end is a trained professional or a fellow student who has been through similar units? | Yes, I would prefer a trained professional. |
| Why do you think a trained professional or peer is better at catering to your problems or answering you? | Professionals are trained for a reason. If I talked to a friend, they might not know how to help properly. A psychology-trained person could better understand, diagnose, and support mental health issues. |
| If you are reading on a community forum that fellow peers are in the same situation as you, how would you feel? | I would probably feel sad and empathetic. I would feel bad that someone else is experiencing the same struggles as me. |

### Section 3: Effortless Intervention (Zero-Friction)

| **Questions** | **Responses** |
|---------------|----------------|
| If we provided a support service without requiring you to make a booking (zero effort), would you still prefer to cope with your problem by yourself, or would you talk through it with someone? | I would definitely talk through it with someone because it feels more personal and helpful to speak directly with one person. |
| What is the maximum amount of time you would be willing to wait for a response before you went back to your usual coping habits? | Honestly, I usually keep problems to myself. If it became really serious, then I would need to talk to someone. |
| What would an ideal "effortless" support experience look like for you? | I would like to talk with someone I know or trust, explain what I am struggling with, and receive guidance on what problems I may be dealing with. |
