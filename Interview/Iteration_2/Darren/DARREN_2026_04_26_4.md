### Section 1: Integrated Access (Blackboard & Discord)

| **Questions** | **Responses** |
|---------------|----------------|
| Do you know that the UQ Student Hub on Discord exists? | Yes, I know it exists, but I have never used it. |
| What do you think if we were to create a mental health forum within the UQ Student Hub on Discord? | I think that sounds interesting. |
| Would you be more likely to use it as someone who reads others' stories or as an active participant posting your own? Why? | I would be someone who reads other people’s stories rather than posting my own. |
| Let’s say you are having a problem mentally; when you imagine a “Support” button directly inside your UQ Blackboard, what do you expect it to do or show? | I would expect it to show a contact person I could talk to, as well as a private space that discusses mental health issues. |

### Section 2: Community vs. Automation

| **Questions** | **Responses** |
|---------------|----------------|
| Does it matter to you if the person on the other end is a trained professional or a fellow student who has been through similar units? | I would prefer a trained professional. However, finding peers with similar mental health issues would also be helpful. |
| Why do you think a trained professional or peer is better at catering to your problems or answering you? | A trained professional would know the solutions because they have studied mental health and know how to help. |
| If you are reading on a community forum that fellow peers are in the same situation as you, how would you feel? | I would feel happy because I would have peers with the same condition as me, and I would not feel alone in my mental health journey. |

### Section 3: Effortless Intervention (Zero-Friction)

| **Questions** | **Responses** |
|---------------|----------------|
| If we provided a support service without requiring you to make a booking (zero effort), would you still prefer to cope with your problem by yourself, or would you talk through it with someone? | I would love to talk to someone about my problems. If that person was a trained professional, it would be even more beneficial. |
| What is the maximum amount of time you would be willing to wait for a response before you went back to your usual coping habits? | One day would be the maximum for me. |
| What would an ideal "effortless" support experience look like for you? | I would like to send a message or talk about my mental health issues and receive a quick response from a trained professional who gives clear steps on what I need to do. I would also like to hear stories from peers who have had similar experiences. |
