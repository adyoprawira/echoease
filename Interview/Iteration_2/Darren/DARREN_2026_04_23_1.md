### Section 1: Integrated Access (Blackboard & Discord)

| **Questions** | **Responses** |
|---------------|----------------|
| Do you know that the UQ Student Hub on Discord exists? | Yes, of course. |
| What do you think if we were to create a mental care forum within the UQ Student Hub on Discord? | I think that would be super helpful because many students have mental health problems and they also want to try new things or seek support. |
| Would you be more likely to use it as someone who reads others' stories or as an active participant posting your own? Why? | I would be more likely to read others’ stories because it creates an environment that supports each other while also being informative. |
| Let’s say you are having a problem mentally; when you imagine a “Support” button directly inside your UQ Blackboard, what do you expect it to do or show? | I would expect something like a chatbot so I could not only see the layout or information, but also have an interactive conversation with the support feature. |

### Section 2: Community vs. Automation

| **Questions** | **Responses** |
|---------------|----------------|
| Does it matter to you if the person on the other end is a trained professional or a fellow student who has been through similar units? | Yes, I would prefer a trained professional because we want help from a professional, not strangers just giving random solutions. |
| Why do you think a trained professional is better at catering to your problems or answering you? | Because they already have expertise in this field, they have handled several people before, and they have studied how to deal with these kinds of problems. |
| If you are reading on a community forum that fellow peers are in the same situation as you, how would you feel? | I think it would be very helpful and supportive because I would know that I am not alone. I would feel like I have someone going through the same experience with me. |

### Section 3: Effortless Intervention (Zero-Friction)

| **Questions** | **Responses** |
|---------------|----------------|
| If we provided a support service without requiring you to make a booking (zero effort), would you still prefer to cope with your problem by yourself, or would you talk through it with someone? | At first, I would try to handle the problem by myself, but if I could not manage it, I would accept help from someone else. |
| What is the maximum amount of time you would be willing to wait for a response before you went back to your usual coping habits? | Probably a maximum of two days. |
| What would an ideal "effortless" support experience look like for you? | For me, effortless support sounds too good to be true, but ideally it would be easy to access, practical, and truly helpful when I need it. |
