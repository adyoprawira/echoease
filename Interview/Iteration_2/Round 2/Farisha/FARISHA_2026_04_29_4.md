## Interview 4

| **Questions** | **Responses** |
|---------------|---------------|
|Imagine you’re on your usual Blackboard dashboard. If there was a button called Support & Well‑being, where would you expect it to be located? | I Would expect it to be located at the end of the main navigation.| 
| If you saw a Support & Well‑being button on Blackboard, what would you expect it to do? | I would expect it to let us enter our problem or chat with a real person.|
|  Would you naturally think of Blackboard as a place to access wellbeing support, or would that feel unexpected?  | I think that would be okay. Blackboard is where we look at courses and resources, which can be overwhelming, so support being there is expected. |
| Imagine you click a support button and land on a page with three options: Read Community Stories, Chat with Someone, and Emergency Resources. What would you think this page is for? |  I think this page is for mental well‑being. |
| Which of those three options would you be most likely to click first, and why? | I would choose Chat with Someone because I can get a real‑time response that could help me. |
| Does that kind of layout feel clear to you, or would you expect something different? | That layout is okay. |
| Imagine there’s an anonymous community where students share short posts about stress, loneliness, exams, or finances. How comfortable would you feel reading posts like that? | I would feel very comfortable because it shows I’m not alone and others face the same problems. |
| Would a read‑only anonymous community feel safe and helpful, or would you prefer being able to reply or interact?  | It is helpful because people can relate or give advice, but personally I would not interact with the posts. | 
| Do you think seeing other students’ experiences would make you feel less alone? | Yes, it would make me feel less alone. | 
|  Imagine you could write an anonymous post about something you’re struggling with. How easy or difficult would it be to share something in that space? | If it’s anonymous, it would be very easy because no one would know it was me. |
| If you could choose up to three topic tags (Stress, Anxiety, Sleep, Relationships), would that help you express what you’re going through? | Yes, that would help. |
| How important is the option to post anonymously for you? | Very important, to make sure I won’t be belittled for what I express. |
| Should we have both anonymous and public posting, or just anonymous? | Just anonymous beause public posts could be misused, people might post made‑up stories under someone else’s name. | 
| In what situation would you actually consider posting something? | When I feel very overwhelmed and want to see if someone can relate. |
| Imagine there’s a private chat where you can talk to a peer mentor or counsellor without booking anything. How likely are you to use something like that? | I would definitely use it. Booking can be a hassle, so if I don’t have to book, I would use it. |
| Would chatting with a peer mentor feel less intimidating than booking a formal counselling appointment? |  Yes, making it casual helps me express myself better. |
| What would make you feel safe or unsafe about using a chat feature for support? | I would feel safe if it offers anonymity or end‑to‑end encryption. |
| If you were stressed late at night, would a chat option feel helpful? | Yes, because my friends would be asleep and I’d have no one to talk to. |
| Imagine there’s a clearly labelled Emergency Resources section inside a wellbeing platform. Where would you expect to find it?  | At the bottom of the page, where settings usually are. |
| What wording would make emergency resources feel clear and trustworthy to you?  | The word “safe” gives me a sense of trust. |
| If you were in a stressful situation, what would help you quickly recognise where urgent help is located?  | A popping colour like yellow or orange, so you know you’re in a problem and can click for immediate help. |
| Would you trust a university platform to direct you to the right emergency contacts? Why? | Yes, because the university is a trustworthy organisation, so they would direct you to trustworthy contacts. |
