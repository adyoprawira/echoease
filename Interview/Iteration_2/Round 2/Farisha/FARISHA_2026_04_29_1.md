## Interview 1
 **Recording link**: [Listen to Interview 1](https://uq.sharepoint.com/:u:/r/teams/Section_7620_22283/Shared%20Documents/54_Richard/Iteration%202_Interview_Nabiha%20Farisha/Round%202/Iteration%202_Round%202_Interview%201_Farisha.m4a?csf=1&web=1&e=yFhBid)

| **Questions** | **Responses** |
|---------------|---------------|
| Imagine you are on your usual Blackboard dashboard. If there was a button called Support & Wellbeing, where would you expect it to be located? | The location isn’t very important, as long as it’s clearly visible to the user. |
| If you saw a Support & Wellbeing button on Blackboard, what would you expect it to do? | I would expect it to let you chat with someone about your wellbeing or check up on students. |
| Would you naturally think of Blackboard as a place to access wellbeing support, or would that feel unexpected? | It feels good that it’s there because many students struggle and need something to help them stay on track. | 
| Imagine you click a support button and land on a page with three options: Community Stories, Chat with Someone, and Emergency Resources. What would you think this page is for? | I would think it’s a page to help students keep peace of mind. |
| Which of those three options would you be most likely to click first, and why? | It depends on my mood. For peace of mind, I’d click Community Stories. If I’m having a hard time and don’t know who to talk to, I’d click Chat with Someone. Emergency Resources would be for urgent situations. |
| Does that kind of layout feel clear to you, or would you expect something different? | It’s clear. I can’t think of anything else to add. |
| Imagine there’s an anonymous community where students share short posts about stress, loneliness, exams, or finances. How comfortable would you feel reading posts like that? | I would relate more, but I’d also feel uncomfortable because those feelings can be contagious and affect my mood. |
| Would a read‑only anonymous community feel safe and helpful, or would you prefer being able to reply or interact? | Interaction is okay if it’s filtered so people can’t say harsh things that harm someone’s mental health. |
| Do you think seeing other students’ experiences would make you feel less alone? | Yes, it would make me feel less alone. |
| Imagine you could write an anonymous post about something you’re struggling with. How easy or difficult would it be to share something in that space? | It would be difficult. People can be touchy, and even if it’s anonymous, comments could make it uncomfortable. |
| If you could choose 2–3 topic tags like Stress, Anxiety, Sleep, or Relationships, would that help you express what you’re going through? | Yes. Anxiety, stress, and relationships are common struggles. | 
| How important is the option to post anonymously for you? In what situation would you consider posting something? | I’d post if I’m having a hard time with assignments, relationship problems, or issues like racism. | 
| Imagine there’s a private chat where you can talk to a peer mentor or counsellor without booking anything. How likely are you to use something like that? | I would definitely use it. |
| Would chatting with a peer mentor feel less intimidating than booking a formal counselling appointment? | Yes. |
| What would make you feel safe or unsafe about using a chat feature for support? | I’d feel safe if they don’t know who I am and my information isn’t stored. It would feel unsafe if data about my feelings is recorded. |
|  If you were stressed late at night, would a chat option feel helpful? | Yes, definitely. |
| Imagine there’s a clearly labelled Emergency Resources section inside a wellbeing platform. Where would you expect to find it? | It should be prominent and very visible once you open the wellbeing section. |
| What wording would make emergency resources feel clear and trustworthy to you? |  Something reassuring, like knowing someone will stay with you instead of sending you to a database. |
| If you were in a stressful situation, what would help you quickly recognise where urgent help is located? | It should be prominent, like a clear label for the Student Health Centre or something similar. |
| Would you trust a university platform to direct you to the right emergency contacts? Why? | Yes, because it’s from UQ, so it should be trustworthy. |
