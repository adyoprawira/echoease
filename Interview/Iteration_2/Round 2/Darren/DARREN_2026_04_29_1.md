## Interview Prototype 1

**Recording link**: 

### Section 1: Blackboard Integration & Navigation

| **Questions** | **Responses** |
|---------------|----------------|
| Imagine you're on your usual Blackboard dashboard. If there was a button called Support & Well-being, where would you expect it to be located? | I would expect it somewhere visible on the main dashboard, maybe on the top menu or sidebar where important tools are usually located. |
| If you saw a Support & Well-being button on Blackboard, what would you expect it to do? | I would expect it to connect me to mental health support, counselling services, emergency contacts, or student wellbeing resources. |
| Would you naturally think of Blackboard as a place to access wellbeing support, or would that feel unexpected? | Since Blackboard is already a platform students use often, having support there would feel natural and make access easier. |
| Imagine you click a support button and land on a page with three options: Read Community Stories, Chat with Someone, and Emergency Resources. What would you think this page is for? | I would think it is a wellbeing support page designed to help students choose the kind of support they need. |
| Which of those three options would you be most likely to click first, and why? | I would choose Chat with Someone first because direct support feels fastest when I am stressed. |
| Does that kind of layout feel clear to you, or would you expect something different? | The layout sounds clear and simple, but icons or short descriptions would make it even better. |

### Section 2: Community Features & Anonymous Sharing

| **Questions** | **Responses** |
|---------------|----------------|
| Imagine there’s an anonymous community where students share short posts about stress, loneliness, exams, or finances. How comfortable would you feel reading posts like that? | I would feel fairly comfortable reading them, especially if it helps me relate to others going through similar situations. |
| Would a read-only anonymous community feel safe and helpful, or would you prefer being able to reply or interact? | A read-only community feels safe, but having the option to reply would make it feel more supportive and interactive. |
| Do you think seeing other students’ experiences would make you feel less alone, or would it not affect you much? | It would make me feel less alone because I would know others are facing similar struggles. |
| Imagine you could write an anonymous post about something you’re struggling with. How easy or difficult do you think it would be to share something in that space? | It would depend on the issue, but anonymous posting would make it much easier to share. |
| If you could choose up to three topic tags (like Stress, Anxiety, Sleep, Relationships), would that help you express what you’re going through? | Yes, topic tags would help a lot because sometimes it is hard to explain feelings clearly. |
| How important is the option to post anonymously for you? In what situations would you actually consider posting something? | Anonymous posting is very important because privacy helps people feel more honest and comfortable. I would post when I feel overwhelmed or need advice. |

### Section 3: Chat Support & Emergency Resources

| **Questions** | **Responses** |
|---------------|----------------|
| Imagine there’s a private chat where you can talk to a peer mentor or counsellor without booking anything. How likely are you to use something like that? | I would probably use it often because it is quick, easy, and does not require appointments. |
| Would chatting with a peer mentor feel less intimidating than booking a formal counselling appointment? What would make you feel safe or unsafe about using a chat feature for support? | Yes, chatting with a peer mentor feels less intimidating than formal counselling. Safety would depend on privacy, professionalism, and clear moderation. |
| If you were stressed late at night, would a chat option feel helpful? | Yes, late-night chat support would feel very helpful because stress can feel worse at night. |
| Imagine there’s a clearly labelled Emergency Resources section inside the wellbeing platform. Where would you expect to find it? | I would expect it at the top of the page, clearly visible, bolded, or highlighted in red. |
| What wording would make emergency resources feel clear and trustworthy to you? | Wording such as “24/7 Emergency Support” or “Urgent Help” would feel clear and trustworthy. |
| If you were in a stressful situation, what would help you quickly recognise where urgent help is located? | Strong colours, visible placement, and simple labels would help me recognise it quickly. |
| Would you trust a university platform to direct you to the right emergency contacts? | Yes, I would trust a university platform more than random websites because it feels more official and reliable. |
