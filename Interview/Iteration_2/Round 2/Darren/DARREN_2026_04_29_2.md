### Section 1: Blackboard Integration & Navigation

| **Questions** | **Responses** |
|---------------|----------------|
| Imagine you're on your usual Blackboard dashboard. If there was a button called Support & Well-being, where would you expect it to be located? | Probably on the dashboard sidebar. |
| If you saw a Support & Well-being button on Blackboard, what would you expect it to do? | I would expect it to open resources or hotlines for mental health support and crisis assistance. |
| Would you naturally think of Blackboard as a place to access wellbeing support, or would that feel unexpected? | It would feel natural because I am already used to using Blackboard, so integrating support there would be convenient. |
| Imagine you click a support button and land on a page with three options: Read Community Stories, Chat with Someone, and Emergency Resources. What would you think this page is for? | I would think it is a page where students can find different options for mental health support. |
| Which of those three options would you be most likely to click first, and why? | It depends on the situation, but in most cases I would choose Read Community Stories first to see other people’s experiences and how they relate to what I am going through. |
| Does that kind of layout feel clear to you, or would you expect something different? | Yes, it feels clear, especially if there are short descriptions for each option. |

### Section 2: Community Features & Anonymous Sharing

| **Questions** | **Responses** |
|---------------|----------------|
| Imagine there’s an anonymous community where students share short posts about stress, loneliness, exams, or finances. How comfortable would you feel reading posts like that? | I would feel comfortable reading them, especially if they are anonymous. |
| Would a read-only anonymous community feel safe and helpful, or would you prefer being able to reply or interact? | I would prefer having the option to reply and interact, as long as I could also remain anonymous when replying. |
| Do you think seeing other students’ experiences would make you feel less alone, or would it not affect you much? | Yes, knowing that other students experience similar struggles would make me feel less alone and less isolated. |
| Imagine you could write an anonymous post about something you’re struggling with. How easy or difficult do you think it would be to share something in that space? | Being anonymous would make me feel safer to post, but it would still depend on the issue. Some topics may still feel too personal to share. |
| If you could choose up to three topic tags (like Stress, Anxiety, Sleep, Relationships), would that help you express what you’re going through? | Yes, topic tags would help because they give others a general idea of what I am experiencing. |
| How important is the option to post anonymously for you? In what situations would you actually consider posting something? | It is very important. I would consider posting depending on the issue, but if it were a deeply personal matter needing professional help, I probably would not post publicly. |

### Section 3: Chat Support & Emergency Resources

| **Questions** | **Responses** |
|---------------|----------------|
| Imagine there’s a private chat where you can talk to a peer mentor or counsellor without booking anything. How likely are you to use something like that? | It depends on the issue. For minor issues, I might use it, especially if I know who I am talking to and their qualifications. |
| Would chatting with a peer mentor feel less intimidating than booking a formal counselling appointment? What would make you feel safe or unsafe about using a chat feature for support? | It feels less formal, but I would need to know who I am speaking to, their qualifications, and that chats are confidential and private. |
| If you were stressed late at night, would a chat option feel helpful? | Maybe. At night when you are tired, sometimes you need someone to talk to, so it could help. |
| Imagine there’s a clearly labelled Emergency Resources section inside the wellbeing platform. Where would you expect to find it? | It should be very visible and one of the first options I see on the page. |
| What wording would make emergency resources feel clear and trustworthy to you? | Clear wording such as “Emergency Contacts Available Now” would feel trustworthy. |
| If you were in a stressful situation, what would help you quickly recognise where urgent help is located? | Clear labels and clearly marked options would help me recognise it quickly. |
| Would you trust a university platform to direct you to the right emergency contacts? | Maybe. It depends on transparency and whether I know I am speaking to a verified professional. If so, I would feel comfortable using it. |
