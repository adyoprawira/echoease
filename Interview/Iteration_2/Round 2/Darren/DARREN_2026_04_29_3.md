### Section 1: Blackboard Integration & Navigation

| **Questions** | **Responses** |
|---------------|----------------|
| Imagine you're on your usual Blackboard dashboard. If there was a button called Support & Well-being, where would you expect it to be located? | I would expect it on the homepage dashboard, maybe near announcements or in the site navigation menu. |
| If you saw a Support & Well-being button on Blackboard, what would you expect it to do? | I would expect it to open wellbeing resources, counselling support, peer support, or general help options. |
| Would you naturally think of Blackboard as a place to access wellbeing support, or would that feel unexpected? | It would feel normal because Blackboard is already part of student life and easy to access. |
| Imagine you click a support button and land on a page with three options: Read Community Stories, Chat with Someone, and Emergency Resources. What would you think this page is for? | I would think it is a support hub where students can choose different ways to get help. |
| Which of those three options would you be most likely to click first, and why? | I would probably choose Read Community Stories first because sometimes I would want to see if others feel the same way. |
| Does that kind of layout feel clear to you, or would you expect something different? | Yes, the layout sounds clear, but adding short explanations under each option would improve it. |

### Section 2: Community Features & Anonymous Sharing

| **Questions** | **Responses** |
|---------------|----------------|
| Imagine there’s an anonymous community where students share short posts about stress, loneliness, exams, or finances. How comfortable would you feel reading posts like that? | I would feel comfortable reading them, especially if they are anonymous and relatable. |
| Would a read-only anonymous community feel safe and helpful, or would you prefer being able to reply or interact? | Read-only feels safe, but being able to react or comment in a controlled way could be better. |
| Do you think seeing other students’ experiences would make you feel less alone, or would it not affect you much? | Yes, it could make me feel more understood and less isolated. |
| Imagine you could write an anonymous post about something you’re struggling with. How easy or difficult do you think it would be to share something in that space? | It would be easier if it is anonymous, but it still depends on how personal the issue is. |
| If you could choose up to three topic tags (like Stress, Anxiety, Sleep, Relationships), would that help you express what you’re going through? | Yes, tags would help because they make it quicker to explain the main problem. |
| How important is the option to post anonymously for you? In what situations would you actually consider posting something? | It is quite important because privacy increases honesty. I would post if I needed advice or wanted support from others. |

### Section 3: Chat Support & Emergency Resources

| **Questions** | **Responses** |
|---------------|----------------|
| Imagine there’s a private chat where you can talk to a peer mentor or counsellor without booking anything. How likely are you to use something like that? | I would likely use it if I needed help quickly and did not want the hassle of booking appointments. |
| Would chatting with a peer mentor feel less intimidating than booking a formal counselling appointment? What would make you feel safe or unsafe about using a chat feature for support? | Yes, peer mentors feel less formal and easier to approach. I would feel safe if chats were private and properly moderated. |
| If you were stressed late at night, would a chat option feel helpful? | Definitely, because stress at night can feel harder when no other support seems available. |
| Imagine there’s a clearly labelled Emergency Resources section inside the wellbeing platform. Where would you expect to find it? | I would expect emergency resources to be the first thing visible or pinned at the top. |
| What wording would make emergency resources feel clear and trustworthy to you? | Something like “Emergency Contacts Available Now” or “Need Immediate Help?” would feel clear and trustworthy. |
| If you were in a stressful situation, what would help you quickly recognise where urgent help is located? | Easy-to-see buttons, clear labels, and separate placement from normal resources would help. |
| Would you trust a university platform to direct you to the right emergency contacts? | Yes, I would trust a university platform because I would assume the contacts are verified and updated. |
