| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| Can you introduce your name and age? | My name is Ili Baharudin, and I am 27 years old. |
| What degree are you taking right now? | I am doing a Bachelor’s in Computer Science, majoring in Cybersecurity. |
| How do you manage your time between school and personal matters? | I use a scheduling system in OneNote where I create a layout of pages for each day of the week. Within each day, I make a to-do list or checklist of tasks I want to complete. For miscellaneous tasks not related to school, I put them in a general box so I can attend to them later if they are not urgent. |
| What do you do to manage your stress? | I try to engage in my hobbies, although it can be difficult when assignments are back-to-back. If I cannot engage in hobbies like arts, I watch dramas and take some time for myself as a form of entertainment outside of my studies. |
| Do you prefer being alone when you are stressed? | It depends on my energy level, but if I am not feeling well, I would prefer having some alone time initially to reflect on my emotions and gain clarity on what I am feeling. |
| Do you have any financial problems? | It is not really financial problems but more about financial commitments. I am not under financial constraint, but I am mindful of saving money, especially for future plans like visiting my elderly parents. |
| On a scale from 1 to 10, how stressed are you about your financial situation? | I would say around 4 to 5. It is not about lacking money, but more about figuring out how to earn more while managing my studies and possibly part-time work. |
| Do you feel like you have enough support around you? | Yes, I would say I do. Financially, I have sponsorship support that provides a stable monthly income. Emotionally, I have supportive housemates, so overall I have a good amount of support. |
| If you are stressed at 2 a.m., who do you turn to? | I would most likely reach out to my housemates first for immediate support. Family may not always be available, and university support services are not immediate, although I would still reach out to them for longer-term support. |
| Have you ever reached out to university support services? | Yes, I have reached out to several services, including financial assistance, mental health support, and legal support. Although there is some waiting time, they are effective once accessed and helpful in handling my situations. |
| If given a choice between talking to a counsellor or taking an anonymous stress quiz, which would you choose? | I would choose to talk to a counsellor because it allows me to express myself more freely. Talking helps me process my emotions better compared to completing a quiz, which requires more thinking and effort. |
| Do you think university support services are safe enough to open up to? | Yes, they emphasise confidentiality and ensure that what is shared remains private. They also give options to remove records if needed, so privacy is not an issue and there is a good level of control. |
