# Test Cards 


## Test Card 1: Discovering the Well‑being Hub

**HYPOTHESIS**
Ask students to start from the dashboard and locate the Well‑being Hub, then explain what they believe the space is for.

**TEST**
Ask students to start from the dashboard and locate the Well‑being Hub, then explain what they believe the space is for.

**METRIC**
- Time taken to locate the hub
- Number of clicks
- Accuracy of their explanation of the hub’s purpose

**CRITERIA**
We are right if:
- 80% of students find the hub in under 3 clicks

- 70% can correctly describe its purpose as emotional/well‑being support

### Interview Question
1. “What would you click if you were stressed today?”

## Test Card 2: Browsing Anonymous Community Posts

**HYPOTHESIS**
Students will feel safe browsing anonymous posts and will understand the tagging system.

**TEST**
Ask students to open the Anonymous Community, read several posts, and interpret the tags.

**METRIC**
- Ability to explain tag meanings
- Comfort level expressed while browsing
- Natural scrolling/engagement behaviour

**CRITERIA**
We are right if:

- 70% correctly interpret tags

- 60% report feeling safe or comfortable in the space

### Interview Question
1. “Do the tags help you find relevant posts?”

## Test Card 3: Creating an Anonymous Post

**HYPOTHESIS**
Students will be able to create a post without confusion and will understand the anonymity toggle.

**TEST**
Ask students to create a post, choose a topic, toggle anonymity, and publish.

**METRIC**
- Number of hesitations or confusions
- Whether anonymity toggle is understood
- Whether moderation messaging is noticed

**CRITERIA**
We are right if:

- 80% complete the post creation flow without assistance

- 70% correctly explain what anonymity does

### Interview Question
1. “Did you feel safe posting this?”

## Test Card 4: Seeking Professional Support

**HYPOTHESIS**
Students will choose a professional based on availability and specialty without confusion.

**TEST**
Ask students to navigate to the professional selection page and choose someone to talk to.

**METRIC**
- Ability to explain their choice
- Understanding of availability labels
- Correct initiation of chat or queue

**CRITERIA**
We are right if:

- 75% correctly interpret availability states

- 70% choose a professional for a clear, logical reason

### Interview Question
1. “What influenced your choice?”

## Test Card 5: Experiencing the Queue Flow

**HYPOTHESIS**
Students will feel calmer and more in control due to the mindfulness tools while waiting.

**TEST**
Ask students to enter the queue, try at least one waiting activity, and enable notifications.

**METRIC**
- Engagement with waiting tools
- Reported anxiety level before vs after
- Understanding of queue position and wait time

**CRITERIA**
We are right if:

- 70% use at least one waiting tool

- 60% report feeling calmer or less bored

### Interview Question
1. “Did the activities help?”

## Test Card 6: Finding Resources in the Directory

**HYPOTHESIS**
Students will quickly locate the resource category they need and understand the available options.

**TEST**
Ask students to navigate to the Resource Directory and find financial aid and after‑hours support.

**METRIC**
- Time taken to find each resource
- Accuracy of their explanation of each card
- Ability to identify crisis support

**CRITERIA**
We are right if:

- 80% find financial aid in under 10 seconds

- 70% correctly identify crisis support

- 70% can summarise what each card offers

### Interview Question
1. “Which resource feels most relevant to you?”

## Test Card 7: Emergency Pathway Stress Test

**HYPOTHESIS**
Students will be able to locate emergency help within 5 seconds, even under simulated stress.

**TEST**
Ask students to imagine they are stressed and locate the emergency help button.

**METRIC**
- Time taken to find the emergency button
- Accuracy of understanding urgent vs non‑urgent options
- Reported trust in the emergency pathway

**CRITERIA**
We are right if:

- 80% locate emergency help within 5 seconds

- 70% correctly distinguish urgent vs non‑urgent options

- 60% report feeling reassured rather than alarmed

### Interview Question
1. “Did this feel urgent enough?”
2. “Are there any features you wish we have?”
