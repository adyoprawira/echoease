# Value Proposition Canvas

## Customer Segment: Students experiencing stress, burnout, and difficulty balancing university and personal life

**Customer Jobs**
- Balance academic responsibilities with personal life
- Manage stress without disrupting study performance
- Find support quickly when overwhelmed
- Maintain well-being while handling deadlines, work, and social commitments

**Customer Pains**
- Formal university support feels too professional or intimidating for personal struggles
- Support services are difficult to access during late-night or high-stress moments
- Reaching out for help can feel like extra emotional effort
- Financial support can feel shameful or uncomfortable to ask for
- Automated tools alone may feel impersonal or unhelpful

**Customer Gains**
- Support that is easy to access at the moment it is needed
- A service that feels safe, approachable, and non-judgmental
- Flexible support options that match different coping preferences
- Help that fits naturally into students’ daily routines
- Greater confidence in seeking support without feeling weak or dependent

**Products and Services**
- A student well-being support platform
- Low-friction access to mental health and financial support pathways
- Anonymous first-contact options
- Hybrid support that combines guided self-help with optional human interaction
- After-hours support features and passive well-being prompts

**Pain Relievers**
- Reduces the formality and psychological barrier of asking for help
- Makes support accessible outside normal office hours
- Provides low-pressure entry points instead of forcing immediate formal appointments
- Reframes financial support in a more empowering and less stigmatized way
- Offers support in a way that feels integrated with everyday student habits

**Gain Creators**
- Creates a student-friendly experience that feels approachable and safe
- Supports both independent self-coping and deeper support when needed
- Increases trust by offering anonymity with the option of human connection
- Encourages earlier help-seeking before stress becomes more serious
- Helps students feel supported without requiring them to leave their normal routines

## Hypothesis Validation

**Accepted Hypothesis**
- Students prefer self-coping unless an intervention is extremely low-friction. This directly supports a value proposition focused on low-friction, everyday, and passive support.

**Refuted Hypothesis**
- Students do not necessarily prefer automated tools such as quizzes over human support. This means the platform should not rely only on automation, and should instead include optional human interaction.

**Partially Accepted Hypotheses**
- Students value centralized services, but centralization alone does not solve the accessibility gap because formal university systems often feel too administrative or inaccessible during personal crises.
- Financial aid is not avoided only because it is hard to access, but also because of stigma, guilt, and self-reliance. This suggests the platform should frame financial support in a more positive and empowering way.
