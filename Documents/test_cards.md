## Test Card

| Hypothesis | Test Method | Success Metric | Criteria |
|------------|-------------|----------------|----------|
| Students will use services more if the process is intuitive and centralized. | Ask 5 students to find the "Financial Aid" page on the uni site while you observe. | Time taken (seconds) + frustration signals. | **Fail** if it takes >60 seconds or they get lost. |
| Anonymity and low-effort entry points will increase service uptake compared to formal appointments. | Show 10 students two options: A) "Talk to a counselor" or B) "Take an anonymous 2-minute stress quiz." | Preference ratio. | **Success** if >80% of students choose the anonymous quiz. |
| Part-time working students avoid financial aid because it isn’t guided or easy to access. | Interview 3 part-time working students: “What’s the one thing stopping you from asking the uni for money?” | Reason tally (most common barriers). | **Success** if “too much effort” or “don’t know how” are top reasons. |
| Students prefer self-coping unless the intervention is extremely low-effort. | Send a simple check-in text to 5 people: “How’s your stress? Want a link to the uni support page?” | Response rate + response type. | **Success** if they decline help (e.g., “I’ll just go for a walk” / “I’ll study more”). |

## 🎤 Interview Questions

| No. | Question |
|-----|----------|
| 1 | How do you manage your time juggling between school and your personal matters? |
| 2 | What do you do to get yourself out of stress? |
| 3 | Is there any point in your life that made you feel like giving up? Why? |
| 4 | When you're struggling with stress at 2:00 AM, where do you turn first? Does school support help you at that hour? |
| 5 | Do you prefer to be alone or talk to someone when you're not in a good mental state? |
| 6 | Do you feel like you currently have enough support around you? |
| 7 | Have you experienced any financial problems? How did you handle them? |
| 8 | On a scale of 1–10, how stressed are you about your debt? |
| 9 | What would make a support service feel “safe” enough for you to open up on the first try? |
| 10 | Do you prefer talking to a counsellor or taking an online quiz? Why? |
| 11 | *(If working part-time)* What’s the one thing stopping you from asking the university for financial help? |
