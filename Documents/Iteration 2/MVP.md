# UQ Student Wellbeing Support Platform

## Problem

Many students experience stress related to academics, finances, loneliness, or mental health, but existing university support services are often difficult to access.

Common barriers identified through interviews include:

- Students do not know where support services are located  
- Searching multiple websites feels tiring  
- Booking appointments feels formal and stressful  
- Many students prefer coping alone  
- Students want help that feels quick, safe, and low-pressure  

---

## MVP Solution

A **Support Button inside Blackboard** that redirects students to a simple student wellbeing platform.

The platform is designed as a **community-first support system**, allowing students to engage anonymously before seeking one-to-one help.

This reduces friction and makes support easier to access through a platform students already use daily.

---

## Core Features

| Feature | Purpose |
|--------|---------|
| Anonymous Forum | Students can read, post, and reply anonymously for peer support |
| Private Chat | Students can privately talk with trained support staff if needed |
| Blackboard Support Button | Provides one-click access through an existing student platform |

---

## Why This Works

Our interviews showed that students often do not reject support itself — they reject friction, formality, and hidden systems.

Key insights included:

- Students feel more comfortable reading shared experiences first  
- Anonymous environments feel safer  
- Peer support feels relatable for everyday stress  
- Professional help is still important for serious issues  
- Easier access increases likelihood of help-seeking  

This MVP addresses those barriers directly.
